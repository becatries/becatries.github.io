SKILL.md
---
name: my-ugc-edit
description: Edit my finished-cut talking-head videos (TikTok/IG, 9:16) in one of my named styles. Use when I hand over a video and ask for an edit.
---

# My UGC edits

I cut my videos myself. You add captions, on-screen text, music, sound effects and a little B-roll on top. Never re-cut or re-time my edit.

## My styles
Use the one I name. If I don't say, use [default style name].
- **[Style 1]**: [e.g. clean and minimal: one-word captions, occasional big emphasis words, soft whooshes]
- **[Style 2]**: [e.g. bright and playful: pop-in text with bounce, stickers, pop sounds]

## Never
- [e.g. flash or light-leak transitions, dark moody grades, italic captions]
- [colors you don't like]

## Captions
- One word at a time. Never two words on screen at once, never stacked.
- Lower third, clear of the app buttons and the description area.
- Font: [font], size [size], color [color]. Emphasis words: [style], about one every 2-3 seconds, never on filler words.
- Follow what I actually said, not the script. Tell me where they differ.
- Spell brand names exactly as the brand does.

## Music
- Royalty-free only. Skip AI-generated tracks and anything registered for copyright claims. No vocals under my voice.
- You pick the track from the brand or video vibe.
- Level: about [17] dB under my voice, ducked when I talk.

## Sound effects
- [e.g. soft whoosh on 2-4 key cuts, pop when text appears]. Always quieter than my voice.

## B-roll
- 2-4 short clips, only where I'm talking about what the clip shows. My clips live in [folder].
- Never cover the product close-ups or the call to action.
- When shots play back to back, cut on my movement so it reads as one motion.

## Reference videos
When I send a reference, copy its structure: what the hook is made of, how long shots hold, the cut rhythm and when my face first appears. If you can't watch it, ask me before editing.

## Brand deals
Read the brief first. Carry exact brand names, required hooks and CTAs, and banned claims into the on-screen text. If my style clashes with the brief, ask me before rendering.

## Before sending
- Check still frames of the hook, every B-roll insert and the ending.
- Check that no text runs off the edges and captions are readable.
- Final loudness around -15 LUFS.
- Tell me what you added, what you skipped and why.

good to know10/10

what helped most

  • give it the real files. a description or screenshot isn't enough to edit from.
  • do your cuts first. i cut in another app and let claude handle captions, text, sound and music.
  • expect a few rounds. by the fourth or fifth video it mostly had my style, because every note got saved.
  • check the brief yourself too. you're the one sending it to the brand.
