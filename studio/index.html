Questions

Good to know

Do I need followers?

No. Trybe is about the videos, not your follower count. Brands run your content as ads.

Do I need Trybe or TikTok Shop already?

No. The studio sets up and runs the Trybe and TikTok Shop accounts for your videos, so you don't need either. If you want to post on your own, keep that on your personal accounts so the money stays easy to track.

How much will I make?

I can't promise a number. It depends on how many brands approve and how well the ads perform. I'll show you exactly what comes in every month.

How do I get paid?

Trybe and TikTok Shop pay the studio accounts. At the end of each month I total everything and send you your 50%, with a statement showing every payout.

What if I want to stop?

Give me 14 days' notice. Videos we already made can keep earning for a while, and the agreement spells out exactly how those get split after we stop. Anything you make on your own after that is 100% yours.

Do you film in person?

To start, only in the Las Vegas area. Everywhere else, you film from home with my scripts and shot lists.

What niches do you take?

I'm strongest in fitness, tech, pets and lifestyle, but tell me yours. If I can sell it, I'm in.
